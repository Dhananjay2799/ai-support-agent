# Support channels and response times

## Response times by plan

The first human response time applies to tickets opened by the assistant or by a customer.

| Plan | Channels | First response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Email, community forum | 72 hours |
| Pro | Email, live chat on weekdays 8:00–20:00 ET | 24 hours |
| Enterprise | Email, live chat, phone, named account manager | 4 hours, around the clock |

Response times are measured from when the ticket is created, not from when the customer
first wrote in.

## When the assistant hands over to a person

The assistant opens a ticket and stops taking actions in the conversation when:

- a refund or credit is above the plan's auto-approve limit
- the customer asks for an exception to a written policy
- it can't find a confident, documented answer to the question
- identity verification fails
- a tool keeps failing after retries
- the customer asks to speak to a person

The customer gets the ticket id (for example `ESC-1042`) and the response time for their plan.
A support specialist picks up the ticket with the full conversation attached, so the customer
doesn't need to repeat themselves.

## Ticket priorities

| Priority | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Urgent | Enterprise account fully unable to access files |
| High | Refunds over the limit, Enterprise billing questions, security concerns |
| Normal | Most questions and policy exceptions |
| Low | Feature requests and general feedback |

## What support can't help with

Support covers Acme Cloud accounts, billing and the product itself. It can't give legal,
tax or financial advice, recover files deleted more than the plan's version-history period
ago, or discuss other companies' products and prices.
