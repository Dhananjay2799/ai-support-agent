# Cancellation and downgrades

## Canceling a Pro subscription
<!-- tiers: pro -->

Pro customers can cancel at any time from **Settings > Billing > Cancel plan**. The plan stays
active until the end of the current billing month, then the workspace moves to the Free plan.
Canceling does not refund the current month automatically; a refund request for that month
follows the normal refund rules.

## Downgrading from Pro to Free
<!-- tiers: pro -->

After the move to Free, the workspace keeps all its files, but:

- If more than 5 GB is stored, the account becomes read-only until usage is under 5 GB.
- Members other than the owner lose access to the workspace.
- Version history older than 7 days is deleted after 30 days.

Tell customers to export anything they need before downgrading if they're over the Free limits.

## Ending an Enterprise contract
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Enterprise contracts renew automatically each year. To end a contract, the customer's admin
gives written notice at least 30 days before the renewal date, through their account manager
or by asking support to open a ticket. Seats can be reduced only at renewal; prorated refunds
for removed seats aren't offered mid-term.

## Reactivating

A canceled Pro workspace can be reactivated at any time by choosing a paid plan again. Files
that were kept on the Free plan are still there; deleted version history can't be restored.
